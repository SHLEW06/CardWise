# Card audit (v7 Part B6)

Audit pass of 2026-11-20 over all 130 cards. The table at the bottom is
generated by `npm run audit:cards` (scripts/card-audit.mjs) from the catalog;
this section is the hand-written record of what the pass checked and changed.

## How this pass was done

1. **Automated consistency checks on every card** (table below): earn keys
   the engine can't map, caps without a matching rate, benefit text that
   mentions bags / free nights / companion fares / lounges with nothing
   modeled, paid hotel cards without a certificate, credits far above the
   fee, portal rates on file.
2. **Issuer pages re-read today** for the cards that drive recommendations
   for the owner-like profile and the ones the brief names, among those
   still marked unverified. Pages that render only with JavaScript (all
   americanexpress.com card pages) returned no terms; those cards stay
   **not verified** — nothing was changed from memory.
3. The 91 cards verified on 2026-11-18 (v6 review) were not re-read.

  (2026-11-20). The brief's example "Bilt → Hyatt / American" doesn't match;
  the partner list stays as is.

  returned from capitalone.com; the page also lists 5x on vacation rentals
  through Capital One Travel. The credit is kept (it's a long-standing
  benefit and absence from a partial fetch isn't evidence) — **unverified**.
  No Global Entry credit or anniversary bonus appeared on the page either.
- **Altitude Connect "4 Priority Pass visits":** the issuer page describes
  the Priority Pass membership but the fetched text has no visit count; the
  v6 review (2026-11-18) recorded four visits from the Business page. Kept,
  **unverified** today.
- **AAdvantage Platinum Select:** the fee and the $125 flight-discount
  threshold didn't render on citi.com; earn rates and the free bag match.
- **Amazon Prime Visa:** chase.com/personal/credit-cards/amazon is a landing
  page with no terms; unverified.
- **US Bank Cash+:** 5% on two chosen categories, combined $2,000 a quarter
  (confirmed). The engine doesn't map "choose your category" keys
  (`Choice5a/5b/2`), so the card earns only its 1% base in the model —
  **known gap**, flagged in the table.
